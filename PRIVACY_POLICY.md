# Privacy Policy for Interview Puzzles

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

**Interview Puzzles** ("the Application") is an interactive educational and technical puzzle simulation app developed by **Suraj** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use the Application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Interview Puzzles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.**

- **Offline / On-Device Storage:** All user profile data (such as candidate name, target company, role, solved puzzle levels, star ratings, and bookmarks) is stored locally on your device using local storage / SQLite. No personal data is transmitted to external servers.
- **No Account Required:** You can use the app as a Guest or with a local offline profile without providing an active email address or phone number.
- **No Background Tracking:** The Application does not track your location, contacts, camera, or sensor data.

---

### 2. Permissions

The Application requires only standard network access:
- **`android.permission.INTERNET`**: Used exclusively for loading standard web assets and fonts. No personal information or tracking metrics are transmitted over the network.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or Mixpanel) or advertising networks (such as AdMob or Unity Ads). Your gameplay and candidate simulation data remains private to your device.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy

The Application is intended for university students, software engineers, and professionals preparing for technical interviews. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 5. Security of Your Data

The security of your data is important to us. Because all data is maintained locally on your device, its protection depends on your device's security controls.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
