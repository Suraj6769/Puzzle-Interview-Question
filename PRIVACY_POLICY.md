# Privacy Policy for Interview Puzzles

**Last Updated: September 14, 2026**

Interview Puzzles ("the Application") is an interactive educational and technical puzzle simulation app developed by **RV Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.** ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

Interview Puzzles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with us or with third parties.

**Offline / On-Device Storage:** The Application may store information locally on your device to provide its features. This may include candidate name, target company, target role, solved puzzle levels, star ratings, bookmarks, gameplay progress, and other locally saved preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

**No Account Required:** You can use the Application as a guest or with a local offline profile. The Application does not require you to provide an email address, phone number, or online account.

**No Background Tracking:** The Application does not track your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or sensor data.

---

### 2. Permissions

The Application may use the following permission:

**`android.permission.INTERNET`**

Internet access is used only when required to load standard web assets, fonts, or other publicly available resources used by the Application.

The Application does not use this permission to collect personal information, track users, or transmit gameplay or candidate simulation data to our servers.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Interview Puzzles does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising services.

The Application does not use services such as:

* Google Analytics
* Firebase Analytics
* Mixpanel
* Google AdMob
* Unity Ads
* Other third-party advertising networks

Your locally stored gameplay and candidate simulation data is not shared with these services.

---

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Interview Puzzles does not maintain a server-side database containing your personal information or gameplay data.

Information stored by the Application, such as your local profile, puzzle progress, solved levels, star ratings, bookmarks, and other locally saved data, remains on your device until it is deleted by you or removed by the Android operating system.

You can generally delete locally stored Application data by:

* Clearing the Application's data through your Android device settings; or
* Uninstalling the Application.

When the Application is uninstalled, locally stored Application data is generally removed according to the behavior of the Android operating system.

Because we do not receive or store this information on our servers, we do not retain copies of your local profile or gameplay data.

If you have questions about data retention or deletion, you can contact us at **[support@rvtech.com](mailto:support@rvtech.com)**.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

Interview Puzzles is designed primarily for university students, software engineers, and professionals preparing for technical interviews.

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the Application does not require an online account and does not transmit personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

---

### 6. Security of Your Data

The security of locally stored information depends primarily on the security controls provided by your Android device.

Because the Application does not transmit your local profile or gameplay data to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database containing this information.

We recommend keeping your device protected with the security features provided by your operating system.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Application, applicable requirements, or our data practices.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated **Last Updated** date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
