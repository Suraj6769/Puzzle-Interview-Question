Privacy Policy

Interview Puzzles

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Interview Puzzles is an interactive technical interview prep and puzzle simulation application developed by Suraj. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of the Application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Interview Puzzles does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).

  • All candidate profiles, target companies, solved puzzle progress, and custom bookmarks are stored strictly on your local device (via local storage).
  • No gameplay or candidate data is sent to external servers or remote databases.

2. Permissions

The Application requests standard android.permission.INTERNET solely to load necessary fonts and web simulation components. No user information or background telemetry is transmitted.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application contains no third-party ad networks (e.g. AdMob) and no behavioral tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit: https://github.com/Suraj6769/Puzzle-Interview-Question
