Privacy Policy for Interview Puzzles

Last Updated: September 14, 2026

Interview Puzzles ("the Application") is an interactive educational and technical puzzle application developed by RV Tech Solutions Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Interview Puzzles application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Interview Puzzles does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with us or with third parties.

The Application may allow users to enter information such as a candidate name, email address, target company, and target role as part of the local interview preparation experience.

Any such information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to our servers.

The Application does not require users to create an online account or provide personal information to use its core functionality.

The Application does not track the user's location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device information.

2. Local Storage and Saved Records

Interview Puzzles may store information locally on your device to provide its features.

This may include:

  • Candidate name
  • Email address entered by the user
  • Target company
  • Target role
  • Solved puzzle levels
  • Star ratings
  • Bookmarks
  • Gameplay and learning progress
  • Other locally saved preferences

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers by the Application.

3. Internet Permission

The Application may use the android.permission.INTERNET permission.

Internet access is used only when required to load standard web assets, fonts, or other publicly available resources used by the Application.

The Application does not use Internet access to transmit your locally stored candidate profile, puzzle progress, or gameplay information to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Interview Puzzles does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising or analytics services for tracking individual users.

The Application does not use services such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or similar advertising and analytics services, unless this policy is updated to reflect a future change.

Locally stored gameplay and candidate simulation data is not shared with third-party analytics or advertising services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Interview Puzzles does not maintain a server-side database containing your locally stored candidate information or gameplay data.

Information stored locally by the Application remains on your device until it is deleted by you or removed by the Android operating system.

You can generally delete locally stored Application data by:

  • Clearing the Application's data through your Android device settings; or
  • Uninstalling the Application.

When the Application is uninstalled, locally stored Application data is generally removed according to the behavior of the Android operating system.

Because we do not receive or store this locally stored information on our servers, we do not retain copies of your local profile or gameplay data.

6. Children's Privacy

Interview Puzzles is designed primarily for university students, software engineers, developers, and professionals preparing for technical interviews.

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the Application does not require an online account and does not transmit locally stored personal information to our servers, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

7. Data Security

The security of locally stored information depends primarily on the security controls provided by your Android device.

Because the Application does not transmit your local profile or gameplay data to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database containing this information.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their Android device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Application, applicable requirements, or our data practices.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Interview Puzzles application, please contact us.
